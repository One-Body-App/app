Siyakwamukela ku-One Body, inkundla yezokuxhumana yobuKristu yokugcina ehlanganisa amakholwa avela emhlabeni wonke. Kungakhathaliseki ukuthi ubheke ukwabelana ngokuqukethwe okukhuthazayo, ukuhlola izimfundiso zenkolo, noma ukuxhumana nomphakathi wesonto langakini, i-One Body ikunikeza okuhlangenwe nakho okungenamthungo nokucebisa ngokomoya.

Izici Eziyinhloko:

Okuphakelayo Okunamandla: Ngena ekusakazweni kokuqukethwe okuklanyelwe okuhlanganisa izindaba, amabhulogi, nezibuyekezo zokuxhumana nabantu ezivela kwamanye amaKristu. I-algorithm yethu ethuthukisiwe iqinisekisa ukuthi ubona okubaluleke kakhulu kuwe.

Ingosi Yomkhuleko Eyodwa: Yabelana ngezicelo zomkhuleko, unikeze ukwesekwa, futhi ugubhe imithandazo ephenduliwe nomphakathi wethu womhlaba. Izwa amandla omthandazo ohlangene kanye nesikhuthazo.

Xhuma futhi Uhlanganyele: Thola futhi ukhuthaze imicimbi, thola amathuba okuzithandela, futhi ujoyine izingxoxo ezibalulekile. Ingosi yethu ye-Connect iyisango lakho lokuya emphakathini wamaKristu onempilo.

Okuqukethwe Okugqugquzelayo Eminweni Yakho: Ngethebhu yethu yokubuka, finyelela iqoqo elibanzi lamavidiyo obuKristu - kusukela ezintshumayelweni kuye ebufakazini, wonke alungele uhambo lwakho lokomoya.

Idizayini Elungele Umsebenzisi: Isixhumi esibonakalayo somzimba owodwa senza ukuzulazula nokusebenzisana nokuqukethwe kube umoya opholile, okuqinisekisa ukugxila kulokho okubaluleke ngempela.

Joyina Umzimba Owodwa namuhla futhi ube yingxenye yomphakathi wamaKristu womhlaba wonke ozinikele okholweni njengawe. Landa manje futhi uqale uhambo lwakho lokuxhumana, ugqozi, nokukhula ngokomoya.
